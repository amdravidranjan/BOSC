BOSC

Bane of Scam Calls

A phone call can push you into giving away a code or sending money. BOSC listens on the phone itself, shows how much pressure the caller is using, and stops a payment made in a panic.

Protected phoneThe phone that gets the call. Listens, warns, guards payments. Guardian phoneA son, daughter or friend. Sees the call's risk live and gets the alert when it ends. Caller phone (demo)Plays the scammer or a genuine caller, with a script to read.

Where the listening happens
On the phone. Speech-to-text and the scam detector run in the browser. Call audio is never uploaded or stored.
What it checks
Eight pressure tactics in the caller's words, your own replies (a code read out, money agreed), whether the voice is computer-made, and whether it matches the family member it claims to be.
What it does about it
Warns in plain words, pauses a payment made under pressure, and sends the call's report to a guardian.

Hackathon prototype. English. Payments are simulated.
